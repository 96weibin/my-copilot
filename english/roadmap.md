# English Learning Roadmap

## Active Route

- focus: workplace English and daily-life English
- default_allocation: 60% workplace / 40% daily life
- adjustment_rule: change the allocation only after diagnostic or four-week checkpoint evidence
- mastery_rule: mark an item stable after two correct, spaced productions in different contexts, or one correct real-world use without prompting

## Workplace English

| Stage | Focus | Exit Evidence | Status | Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Accurate short sentences: grammar, UI actions, and product wording | Correct core patterns in two different productions | Active | Complete workplace diagnostic |
| 2 | Meeting flow: opening, transition, clarification, risk, follow-up, and close | Handle a short meeting simulation with controlled errors | Planned | Use diagnostic gaps to select practice |
| 3 | Demo narration and collaboration writing | Explain a familiar flow and send concise updates without repeated core errors | Planned | Start after Stage 2 evidence |
| 4 | Real-work transfer | Use target patterns correctly in real work without prompting | Planned | Review real-use evidence |

## Daily-Life English

| Stage | Focus | Exit Evidence | Status | Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Baseline: comprehension, practical interaction, and personal expression | Complete daily-life diagnostic and identify prioritized gaps | Not started | Start after workplace baseline |
| 2 | High-frequency routines, preferences, plans, and social responses | Produce short, natural messages and conversations in two contexts | Planned | Select gaps from diagnostic |
| 3 | Practical situations: food, shopping, travel, directions, appointments, and problems | Complete short role-play interactions with understandable, accurate responses | Planned | Build from Stage 2 material |
| 4 | Everyday opinions and experiences | Sustain a familiar conversation and self-correct common errors | Planned | Review real-life practice evidence |

## First Cycle

1. Complete the workplace diagnostic.
2. Complete the daily-life diagnostic.
3. Use both baselines to create a two-week task sequence within the weekly time budget.
4. Run a comparable checkpoint after four weeks of completed practice.
